NEON REQUIEM TACTICS

A roguelike tactics run through the chrome-and-sorcery undercity of Vel'Kesh. Every soldier is born from a song: drop a Spotify track link and its genre becomes their class, its tempo becomes their body.

How it plays

  • Isometric grid battles. Units act by Charge Time: faster units act more often.
  • Each turn: Move once and Act once (attack or class skill), then Wait.
  • High ground gives +20% damage; hitting someone from behind gives +50%.
  • Fallen allies are lost for good, unless an Idol revives them mid-battle.
  • Clear 8 sectors and defeat the Archon to win the run.

Overrides & Spotify API token (optional)

Without a token, the game reads the track title and cover art from Spotify's public embed info and derives genre and BPM from the track ID itself, so the same song always makes the same character. Spotify no longer gives tempo to most new API apps, so BPM falls back to the ID unless you enter it.
